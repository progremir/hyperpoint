Privacy

Last updated 29 October 2026.

The app

Hyperpoint does not collect, send or sell anything. It reads the keyboards and mice connected to your Mac and one settings file, and it writes only to your devices and that file.

Its one network request asks this site whether a newer version is out. It makes it once a day if you allowed automatic checks when Hyperpoint asked, and whenever you choose Check for Updates. The request carries the app's version and your IP address, like any web request, and nothing about you, your devices or your settings. You can turn the daily check off in Settings › General.

Your settings live in ~/.config/hyperpoint/config.json. The record of what Hyperpoint did, like a device connecting or settings being applied again, goes to the macOS log on your Mac and nowhere else.

macOS asks you to grant Accessibility and Input Monitoring. Hyperpoint uses them to change keys, scrolls and mouse buttons as they happen. It does not record what you type.

This website

The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Its fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address when your browser asks for them. Downloads and updates come from this site, and Cloudflare, which hosts it, logs the requests for them. Release notes are served by GitHub, under GitHub's privacy statement.
