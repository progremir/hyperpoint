What we heard

The brief

A menu-bar app for macOS that replaces BetterMouse and Karabiner for one person's setup: MX Master buttons over HID++, a hyper key on Caps Lock, smooth scrolling with a tuned curve, and every setting re-applied the moment a device reconnects. It should feel precise, quiet and native.

precisequietnativetactile

Success: reads as the Caps Lock key and a pointer at once, survives as an 18 pt menu-bar template icon, works in one colour.

Concept A

Caps pointer

The Caps Lock glyph tipped over 22 degrees until it becomes a pointer.

  • Caps Lock is the key Hyperpoint turns into hyper; the pointer is what it drives
  • Outlined like the real ⇪ legend, so it reads as a key glyph
  • The bar is the accent: the key's lit LED
  • Tilt makes it lively but costs clarity below 24 px
