What we heard

The brief

A menu-bar app for macOS that replaces BetterMouse and Karabiner for one person's setup: MX Master buttons over HID++, a hyper key on Caps Lock, smooth scrolling with a tuned curve, and every setting re-applied the moment a device reconnects. It should feel precise, quiet and a little cheeky, not like another blue system-utility gear.

precisequietcheekytactile

Success: reads as a rat (the mouse pun) at a glance, survives as an 18 pt menu-bar template icon, works in one colour, and stays clear of Mickey-style round-ear mascots.

Concept A

Ratchet rat

Ratchet, rat, mouse: a rat's face built from one long wedge, two ears and two eyes.

  • The pun does the explaining: a mouse utility named after a rat
  • Long snout and small ears keep it rat, not Mickey
  • Pink ears are the one colour accent; everything else is ink
  • Small cut with bigger eyes holds up in the 18 pt menu bar
